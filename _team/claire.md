Claire is a highly regarded college counselor with more than four decades of experience guiding students and families through the college admissions process. She has helped students gain admission to many of the all of the selective universities in the United States and many others across the world.

Claire’s approach is highly personalized, strategic, and student-centered. She takes the time to understand each student’s strengths, interests, aspirations, and unique story, then develops a comprehensive, step-by-
step strategy designed to present the strongest and most authentic application possible. She provides thoughtful guidance and hands-on support throughout every stage of the process—from shaping an academic and extracurricular profile and identifying best-fit colleges to developing compelling essays and presenting a cohesive, distinctive application.

With extensive experience working as the Director of College Counseling at prestigious high schools and with a master’s degree in secondary school counseling, Claire brings a comprehensive perspective and nuanced insight to an increasingly complex and competitive admissions landscape. Her extensive experience with Admission officers has given her a deep
understanding of what individual colleges value and how to help students position themselves thoughtfully and authentically. She helps students and families approach the process with confidence, clarity, and purpose.

Families consistently describe Claire as someone who goes above and beyond, providing exceptional personal attention, thoughtful guidance, and unwavering support throughout the process. In keeping with EAG’s mission, Claire’s role extends well beyond helping students gain admission. She helps each student identify what makes them distinctive, articulate those qualities authentically, and find colleges where their talents, interests, and goals will allow them to thrive.
