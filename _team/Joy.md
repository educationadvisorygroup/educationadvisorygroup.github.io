Joy has over 17 years of experience as a college counselor, working with students applying to a broad range of institutions, including highly selective universities, top liberal arts colleges, and diverse public and private schools across the country.
 
She has a master’s degree in social work and she draws on this background and her past experience in the medical field to support students academically and personally during one of the most important transitions of their lives. She has spent her career helping students identify colleges and universities that align with their academic interests, personal values, and long-term aspirations.
 
 
Known for building meaningful relationships with her students, Joy's encouraging approach instills confidence, helping her students navigate the admissions process skillfully and with confidence. Having toured over 250 schools, she brings firsthand knowledge to every conversation about fit and values, building strong, lasting relationships with students and their families throughout the process
 
She guides her students through the admissions process, working with them to uncover their academic and extracurricular passions and connect those interests to potential majors and career paths. In a typical first meeting, Joy creates a welcoming space where students can share their backgrounds, goals, and any concerns they might have about the college process. Throughout the experience, she asks thoughtful questions to get to know each student’s unique interests and goals and together they begin to identify priorities for the college search. Her focus is on helping students find colleges that not only support their aspirations but also foster their growth.
 
Joy's students have been successfully admitted to some of US and Canada's most selective colleges where they have discovered strong communities and a true sense of belonging. They have majored in CS, Engineering, Economics, Business, English, humanities and the Arts.
