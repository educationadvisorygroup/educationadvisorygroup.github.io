With more than 30 years of experience working with
teenagers and families, Joann brings a rare combination
of college counseling expertise, professional counseling
skills, and a deep understanding of the challenges today’s
students face. Her enthusiasm for working with students is
matched by her commitment to helping each young
person discover their strengths, build confidence, and find
the path that is right for them. 

For more than 20 years, Joann served as a high school
counselor, working with students across the full academic
and emotional spectrum. She understands that every
student is different—and that successful college
counseling is about far more than grades, test scores, and
a list of colleges. It is about helping students understand
themselves, recognize their potential, develop the skills to
advocate for themselves, and make thoughtful decisions
about their future. 

Joann has also served as a college application reader at a
prominent university, giving her valuable insight into how
admissions decisions are made and what truly
distinguishes an application. Her extensive involvement in
her state’s professional college counseling association has
kept her at the forefront of evolving admissions practices
and trends. 

As a licensed professional counselor and published
author, Joann has dedicated much of her career to
helping students and families successfully navigate the
college process. She is the author of When to Do What: A Step-by-Step Guide to the College Process and The
Honeycomb Approach: 6 Factors to Find Your College Fit,
providing families with practical, thoughtful guidance
throughout the journey. 

Joann’s greatest strengths—her “superpowers”—are her
ability to listen deeply, build students’ self-confidence, and
bring organization and clarity to what can otherwise feel
like an overwhelming process. She creates a supportive
relationship in which students feel heard, understood, and
empowered to take ownership of their college journey. 

For students, Joann is a trusted advocate and
encouraging mentor. For parents, she is an experienced
professional who brings perspective, structure, and
reassurance to one of the most important transitions in a
young person’s life.
